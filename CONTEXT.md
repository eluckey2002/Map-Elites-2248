# 2248 Game Language

This glossary defines the player-facing concepts used to describe tiles and their progression.

## Language

**Family**:
A recognized doubling progression `root × 2ⁿ`, anchored at 2, 3, 5, 7, or 9 and represented by a shared color. Family color communicates progression compatibility; ancestry and divisibility alone do not determine membership.
_Avoid_: Lineage, difficulty class, ruleset, tile type

**Progression**:
The doubling sequence within any family. Progression math is identical across families.
_Avoid_: Family level, family mechanic

**Non-family compound**:
A value that does not lie on one of the five recognized family progressions. It uses the neutral silver treatment even though it can still participate in legal equal-or-double chains with matching values.
_Avoid_: New family, mixed family, inherited family

**Family mix**:
The combination and placement of families on a board. A family mix creates tactical pressure when one sequence impedes another and creates opportunity when its placement supports a valuable legal chain; the families themselves still use identical rules.
_Avoid_: Difficulty of a family

**Material stage**:
The visual form associated with a value's advancement, such as granules, compound, crystal, or potion. The same material stages apply to every family.
_Avoid_: Family, tier rules
