## Source findings

1. **Initial and refill spawns are on-lattice.** `solver/engine.js:22-27` emits `2x`, `4x`, `8x`, or `16x` the tile scale initially; `solver/engine.js:142-146` emits `2x`, `4x`, or `8x` on refill. Neither mechanic creates an off-lattice value.
2. **The ordinary preference is not a prohibition.** `scoreGreedyPath` searches for a mergeable prefix, but when none exists it returns the full path at `solver/engine.js:322-338`. Those fallback paths enter the shared candidate list, which is sorted and selected by the normal policy at `solver/engine.js:470-495` and `solver/bot.js:341-362`.
3. **A target-reaching override deliberately disables trimming.** `immediateWinningUntrimmed` calls the generator with `preferMergeableSum: false` and supersedes the ordinary choice at `solver/bot.js:369-385`. An off-lattice remnant can be rational there because the browser ends the level immediately at the target (`src/game.js:667-673`).
4. **Bomb handling also bypasses the preference.** A bomb causes `chooseBaseMove` to take an exhaustive chain ending at the bomb through `findBestChain`, without a mergeable-sum restriction (`solver/bot.js:335-339`).
5. **“Nothing will ever match” is too strong.** The comment at `solver/engine.js:258-262` says an off-lattice sum can never be matched, but the live chain rule permits any two equal starting tiles (`solver/engine.js:68-80`). Spawns will not provide a matching off-lattice value, but a separately created equal value could match it. The current boolean measures absence from the spawn lattice, not literal permanent unusability.
6. **The evaluator continues after the live game would end.** `playToBudget` explicitly does not stop at the target (`solver/policy-eval.js:45-76`), while the browser does. This is appropriate for uncensored score evaluation but not automatically appropriate for a human-experienced pressure descriptor.

## Append-only correction — 2026-09-02 measurement audit

The sentence above saying `2,844` off-lattice cells across `46,928` playable-cell observations “reproduce overall panel pressure `0.06060347766791681`” used a different aggregation from the production descriptor and must not be read as a reproduction claim. It is a **cell-weighted diagnostic ratio**. Production first calculates one fraction per move and then averages the move fractions; because levels have different non-stone footprints, its equal-observation panel value is `0.06205316319536222` (the mean of the four equally observed policy aggregates), not `0.06060347766791681`. The raw origin and timing counts remain unchanged, as do percentages whose denominator is the `2,844` off-lattice cell-observations. This correction supersedes only the mislabeled aggregate sentence and preserves it above for auditability.
