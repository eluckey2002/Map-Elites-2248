## Objective

The level 52 target decision is decidable from measured numbers rather than
projection: what its target would become against the current bot, what that
costs a player, and what the bot win rate says — with nothing in the repo
changed.

This ticket produces evidence. The decision itself is the owner's and is not
taken here.

## Fixed inputs

- Level 52 **ships**: `src/game.js` holds levels 1..52, verified by parse. Its
  shipped target is 102000.
- Level 52 as recorded by the old bot (`solver/candidate-levels-52.receipt.json`,
  now stale): `measuredMedian` 146688, `demand` 0.70, holdout **290/300 wins
  (96.7%)**, 0 lockouts, 0 bombs, 10 out-of-moves.
- Target policy: `median x demand`, rounded down by `roundTarget`.
- Measured precedent from T-002: re-authoring level 54 against the current bot
  moved its target +6.7% (150000 -> 160000) and its median +6.5%.
- Shape manifest: `solver/candidate-shapes/level-52-stone-gate.json`.
- Hazard, as in T-002: `author-level.js --write` writes to the hardcoded
  `candidate-levels.*` names, so measuring requires backing up the champion and
  restoring it. Here the level 52 output must ALSO be discarded — this ticket
  measures, it does not re-author.

## Completion test

All oracles `pre-existing`: specified before any command runs.

1. **Level 52's would-be target is measured, not extrapolated.**
   oracle: an `author-level.js --shape solver/candidate-shapes/level-52-stone-gate.json --write`
   run against the current bot; its derived `target` and `measuredMedian` read
   from the produced files and quoted in `## Result

Measured against the current bot (authored, read, then discarded; nothing kept):

| | shipped / old bot | current bot | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| target | 102000 | **107000** | +5000 (**+4.9%**) |
| measuredMedian | 146688 | 153792 | +4.8% |
| holdout wins | 290/300 (96.7%) | **291/300 (97.0%)** | **+0.3 points** |

**The decisive number is the win rate, and it did not move.** The bot won 96.7%
of holdout seeds on level 52 before and 97.0% now — one extra win in 300. Its
median *score* rose 4.8%, but its ability to beat the level was already
saturated and still is. Level 52 was never hard for the bot, by construction:
`demand: 0.70` sets the target at 70% of what the bot typically scores.

So re-targeting would raise the bar **4.9% for a human player**, justified by a
metric that is not measuring this level's difficulty. Nothing observable about
the level got easier.

### Options

**(a) Re-target to 107000.** Gate goes green. Level 52 becomes 4.9% harder for
every player, on the basis of a median score gain, while the win-rate evidence
says the level's difficulty is unchanged. Irreversible in the sense that players
experience it.

**(b) Leave the target at 102000, accept one honest red.** Nothing changes for
players. The suite carries a permanent single failure saying "level 52's receipt
predates the current bot", which is true. Costs: a habitually red suite, which
the receipt card already records as a blind spot.

**(c) Fix the coupling that forces the choice.** The receipt derives target from
a fresh median and asserts they agree, so a shipped level can never hold a
frozen target and verify. A `targetPolicy: frozen-at-playtest` variant would let
a shipped level record its playtested target, still re-verify shape and current
measurement, and stop generating false debt every time the bot improves. This is
the actual defect; (a) and (b) are both workarounds for it.

### Recommendation

**(b) now, (c) when there is appetite for design work.** Do not take (a): it
degrades a shipped level for a player on evidence that does not say the level
changed. (c) is the real fix and would retire this entire class — every future
bot improvement currently manufactures fresh "stale" receipts for every shipped
level, so this recurs by design, not by accident.

**The choice is the owner's; this ticket takes no decision.**

## Feedback

- Reusing T-002's measured +6.7% as level 52's expected delta would have been
  wrong by nearly two points (actual +4.9%). Per-level medians move differently;
  a percentage measured on one level is not evidence about another. Criterion 1
  forbade the shortcut on purpose and the forbidding paid.
- The same backup/restore workaround as T-002 was needed again — third occurrence
  of the hardcoded-output-path friction in one session.

## Risks

- The recommendation rests on bot win rate as the difficulty signal. All human
  evidence on this project is n=1, so "unchanged for the bot" is not proof of
  "unchanged for a person". It is the best available evidence, not conclusive.
- Option (c) touches the receipt derivation, which every existing receipt
  depends on. It would need its own spec and a gate-check pass, not an edit.
