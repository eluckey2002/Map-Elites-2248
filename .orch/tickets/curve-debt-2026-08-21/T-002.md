## Feedback

- **Cut defect, not an executor defect.** This ticket's `excluded_actions`
  forbade "modifying solver/candidate-levels.json", while its own Fixed inputs
  documented that `author-level.js --write` unavoidably writes there. Per
  contracts/work-item.md, an item forbidden to touch what it must touch cannot
  be executed as written and the contradiction belongs to the cut. The invariant
  actually worth protecting is the end state, and criterion 2 tested exactly
  that. Better wording for a future cut: "leaving candidate-levels.json
  modified", not "modifying" it.
- **Mechanization candidate.** The backup/relocate/restore dance exists only
  because `author-level.js` hardcodes `STORE_PATH` and `RECEIPT_PATH`. An
  `--out <basename>` flag would remove the workaround and the risk of clobbering
  the one passing receipt. Second occurrence of this manual dance in one session.
- **Evidence for T-003.** This run measured the effect the level 52 decision
  turns on: a re-author against the current bot raised a target by 6.7%. That is
  no longer a projection.
