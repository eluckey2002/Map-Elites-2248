## Result

Files changed: `solver/tests/receiptGate.test.js`, `docs/CHECK-CARDS.md`.
No hashed input touched, so no receipt was invalidated.

The gate now computes an exemption per store and routes to one of three
verdicts (`gateVerdict`): `current`, `stale-exempt` (diagnostic, no failure), or
`stale-blocking` (assertion). Exemption requires BOTH halves, computed fresh on
every run from `src/game.js` and `recordings/`:
- the candidate's `level` appears in the shipped level table, AND
- a recording binds to its exact `candidateIdentity` with `outcome: "win"`.

Level 52 qualifies and is reported, not failed. Levels 53 and 54 are current and
pass outright; neither ships, so neither could ever be exempt.

**What the exemption does not catch** — the reason it is the most attackable
part of this change:
- It never expires. One winning recording licenses it forever, on n=1 evidence.
- Level 52's behaviour under the current bot is unknown and stays unknown.
- It converts a loud failure into a quiet diagnostic; a diagnostic is worth only
  as much as the person reading the output.
- `shippedLevels()` regex-parses `src/game.js`; a format change silently shrinks
  the set. That fails closed (more blocking, not less), but silently.
- `winningRecordingIdentities()` trusts the recorded `outcome` without replaying.

## Feedback

- The decisive fact was found by probing, not reasoning: appending one comment to
  `solver/level-author.js` flipped both passing receipts to
  `code/input identity mismatch`. That single experiment invalidated a 12-criterion
  spec I had written and stamped an hour earlier, because delivering it would have
  forced re-authoring level 52 — the exact action the owner had just declined.
  The spec's own `risks` gestured at "schema change" but never named the hash
  coupling. A spec that touches a hashed input should state, as a binding
  constraint, what that invalidates.
- `orch-spec` has no step that asks "what does delivering this break?" — the
  acceptance criteria describe the end state, and `risks` is prose. A stamped
  spec can be internally perfect and still self-defeating.

## Risks

- The exemption is a place debt can go to be forgotten. The card names the
  signal: a stale count that never falls means the mechanism has become a
  parking space rather than a report.
- Level 52 now has no live characterisation and no scheduled reason to acquire
  one. That is the accepted cost of not degrading shipped content.
