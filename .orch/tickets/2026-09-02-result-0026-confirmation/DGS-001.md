## Accepted Goal Contract

Qualify a mechanized policy-comparison gate with a Challenge Receipt, then run
one preregistered fresh confirmation of the frozen handmade policy against the
current reference bot. Preserve raw results and require downstream admission to
consume the receipt.

## Proof map

1. A committed protocol predates any reportable cell and freezes the policy,
   comparator, engine, runner, verifier, independent arithmetic, admission
   consumer, levels, seeds, outcomes, and stopping rules.
2. The same verifier passes a real qualification artifact, rejects a controlled
   wrong-outcome twin, and invalidates a covered source-identity mutation.
3. Confirmation refuses to run without the exact Challenge Receipt; admission
   refuses to issue without that receipt, the confirmation's recorded
   consumption, full deterministic replay, and independent arithmetic agreement.
4. Exactly one reportable confirmation artifact is produced on the registered
   fresh range, preserving every paired raw cell and its move trace.
5. The report and ledger retain the empirical verdict exactly as computed,
   including an honest `INCONCLUSIVE` or `FALSIFIED` outcome.

## Constraints

- Do not promote or modify the production chooser.
- Do not tune the handmade policy or inspect the fresh outcomes before the
  protocol and qualification receipt are committed.
- Treat `6000000..6000024` and `7000000..7000024` as burned.
- Use the real shipped move budget; a loss penalty affects analysis only and
  never grants extra gameplay.
- Preserve unrelated tracked and untracked work.

one Check Card, a real qualification artifact, and Challenge Receipt
`56b8b29e...`. The receipt passed the real burned-seed subject, failed a
wrong-outcome twin, and failed a covered source-identity twin.
