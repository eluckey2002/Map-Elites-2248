## Result

### Ranked findings

1. **HIGH — `Require:` has no named T0 carriers.** The contract accepts
   "one named instrument, its intended purpose, its proposed authority rung,
   fixed evidence sources, and a review bound" as bare prose
   (`instrument-check-card/SKILL.md:7-8`). Composition law requires every
   `Require:` item to ride a named field defined by a T0 contract, never bare
   prose (`composition.md:42-44`). No T0 contract or carrier names occur in the
   fixed skill or template. This makes dispatch input mapping non-contractual,
   especially for the evidence set and review bound.

2. **HIGH — the return has two owners for four facts.** The procedure requires
   completing the card (`SKILL.md:15`), and the card itself contains all control
   results (`card-template.md:27-31`), boundary findings, recommended rung, and
   gate decision (`card-template.md:42-46`). `Return:` then emits `card` plus
   separate `control_results`, `boundary_findings`, `recommended_rung`, and
   `gate_decision` (`SKILL.md:32-33`) without an equality or projection rule.
   This violates the library-lens invariant that every fact is stated exactly
   once (`library-lens.md:5-8`) and permits the card and sibling fields to
   disagree at the gate boundary.

3. **HIGH — result-state ambiguity can leak unsupported authority.** The skill
   permits an unrun *or* inconclusive control to be either `UNVERIFIED` or
   `UNKNOWN` (`SKILL.md:17-19`), while the template directs `UNKNOWN` whenever
   fixed evidence does not decide a field (`card-template.md:3-5`) and leaves
   the exact vocabulary to each completed card (`card-template.md:30`). The
   only explicit high-authority veto is for an "unverified" load-bearing
   control (`SKILL.md:24-25`); failed, inconclusive, and `UNKNOWN` load-bearing
   controls are not mapped explicitly to that veto. Because the skill may
   recommend blocking or automatic authority, this reference inconsistency is
   load-bearing rather than editorial.

4. **MEDIUM — the fixed-source/review boundary is neither enforced nor
   receipted.** Fixed evidence sources and a review bound are required
   (`SKILL.md:7-8`), but the procedure says to inspect "the instrument and its
   real consumers" and complete the card from primary evidence
   (`SKILL.md:10-16`) without saying those reads remain inside the supplied
   source set and bound. Neither the template nor `Return:` records an evidence
   manifest or the review bound. Per composition law, a method substitution may
   not relax a bound (`composition.md:49-55`); the fixed contract supplies no
   durable field by which a caller can check that invariant.

5. **MEDIUM — the template promises an unnamed downstream gate and leaves the
   consumer edge unresolved.** "The later gate check compares this card with
   the proposed use and makes the proceed, restrict, or stop decision"
   (`card-template.md:48-49`) asserts the existence and semantics of external
   enforcement, but no gate implementation, call edge, or named carrier is in
   the fixed corpus. That conflicts with the library lens's ban on incidental
   promises and prose enforcement claims (`library-lens.md:20-22`) and leaves
   the returned card without a concrete consumer under composition law
   (`composition.md:47-48`). The exact sentence I would delete is: **"The later
   gate check compares this card with the proposed use and makes the proceed,
   restrict, or stop decision."** The adjacent sentence that the card never
   approves itself is the useful contract boundary.

6. **MEDIUM — failure paths do not contractually return partial evidence.** The
   skill handles unrun or inconclusive *controls* (`SKILL.md:17-19`), but says
   nothing about source-read, parse, consumer-resolution, or execution failure
   after work has begun. Its fixed `Return:` shape (`SKILL.md:32-33`) has no
   partial-result/evidence rule. Composition law requires every failure path to
   return partial results and gathered evidence (`composition.md:35-36`).
