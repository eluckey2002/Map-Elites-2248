## Existing capabilities

- `solver/record-session.js` already runs one deterministic bot game through the same terminal lifecycle as the browser and records `boardBefore`, the chosen chain's coordinates and values, immediate points, score after each move, and the final board. Its own comment says sessions can be replayed/viewed later.
- The capability is live: Level 52, seed `2000000` produced a 12-move win scoring `109,440/102,000` and wrote an exact session to `/private/tmp/2248-bot-session-level52-seed2000000.json`.
- No renderer consumes this session format. The repository has no bot autoplay or playback page. `src/index.html` is human input only; `solver/recording-replay.js` verifies human recordings headlessly but does not display them.

## Available fields

- Already recorded: level, seed, grid dimensions, move budget, target, tile scale, board before each move, chosen chain, chain length, chain tile sum (derivable), immediate points, score after the move, final board, and live terminal outcome.
- Already computed internally but discarded: `rolloutValue` calculates the best predicted next-move points after each candidate; `simulateCandidate` retains the survivor and simulated afterstate; the chooser computes immediate points, predicted next points, survivor-chainability value, turnover value, harvest value, and total candidate score.

## Missing fields

- The exact candidate set and each candidate's component scores.
- The predicted next chain itself; current `rolloutValue` returns only its points.
- Highest-immediate and longest-chain alternatives as distinct comparisons.
- Immediate chain sum versus scored points as separately labeled values.
- Predicted two-move total and the actual next move shown side by side.
- Survivor value and post-gravity location in the emitted session.
- Policy parameters/identity and code identities in the recording.
- Any visual, step, play/pause, speed, or chain-highlighting surface.

## Smallest next slice

Build one owner-facing playback for one exact level, seed, and policy by reusing `recordSession` rather than adding an experiment runner:

1. step/play/pause the recorded real terminal game;
2. render the board before and after each move and highlight the chosen chain;
3. show chosen chain length, raw tile sum, immediate points, survivor value/location, and score progress;
4. show three explicitly different alternatives: longest available chain, highest immediate-points chain, and highest policy-total candidate;
5. show the chosen candidate's predicted best next chain and `move 1 points + predicted move 2 points`, beside the actual next move when it becomes available;
6. pin level, seed, policy identity, and relevant code identities;
7. make no claim that any displayed field is the right descriptor until the owner watches the game and accepts its meaning.

This is an inspection tool, not an experiment and not an evidence-admission gate.

## Gaps

- “Best” must remain split into longest, highest immediate score, and highest policy-total; collapsing them would repeat the semantic mistake under a new label.
- The bot's forecast uses a deterministic lookahead spawn stream, which can differ from the actual next spawn. Bot Vision must label predicted and actual paths rather than implying foresight of the real random outcome.
- No UI design or implementation was authorized by this inventory ticket; the owner accepted the concept and name, not a frozen visual specification.
