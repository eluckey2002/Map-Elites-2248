## Contradictions

- Coverage terminology in the colleague report collapses two different measures. The accepted current corpus result is production `2/39` **exact** and `4/39` action-equivalent; targeted generator `5/39` exact and `20/39` action-equivalent. `compare_recordings.js` checks only ordered coordinate-path equality, so its `37/39 absent` claim means exact-path absence, not action-equivalent absence.
- “Any chain crossing the target” is broader than the implementation. The immediate-win branch only searches the reference generator's bounded untrimmed pool (`limit: params.width`); it is not exhaustive over all legal chains.
- The README says the survivor “lands next to” big tiles, but the tie-break is evaluated on the last cell before execution, gravity, and refill. The source comment and colleague message qualify this as pre-gravity; it must not be interpreted as actual post-transition placement.
- The project convention visible in the current reference-bot evidence clusters uncertainty by level. `measure.js` computes its `t` over all 225 game differences as independent cells. Therefore `t = 2.69` is the program's reproducible statistic, not yet the project's accepted inferential statistic.
