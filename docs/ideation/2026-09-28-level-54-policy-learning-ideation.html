What Level 54 is actually teaching us

The next useful question is not “which board bonus should we add?” It is “what makes stored board value convertible, and over what sequence should a choice be judged?”

Date: 2026-09-28 Topic: level-54-policy-learning Focus: sharp next investigations Mode: repo-grounded
Central reflection

Your play looks less like a series of independently good moves and more like a short policy: take temporary debt, preserve a convertible structure, then harvest it. Our recent labels mostly judged the pieces instead of the policy.

Codebase Context

Level 51 produced the current target-aware champion. Level 54 then exposed a remaining difference: the owner completed the seeded game in 15 moves, the champion in 23, and the exact oracle witness in 14. The owner’s second move was absent from the champion’s candidate pool; route diversification recovered it, but the current scorer rejected it and the broad challenger was too expensive.

Three successive candidate explanations did not survive broad evidence. Continuation density caused 24 champion-win regressions. A built-reservoir proxy missed 5 of 10 labeled decisions. Exact three-step target progress missed 6 of 10, including four cases whose ordering reversed just beyond the horizon. The four-miss diagnostic showed that eventual winners held more ready reservoir at the short horizon—but move 8 is a direct counterexample to treating reservoir quantity as the answer.

The decisive contrast

Move 6 · helpful Owner wins 15 vs 19

At three steps, the owner is 9,024 points behind but has 8,192 more reservoir points.

Move 8 · harmful Owner loses 17 vs 15

At three steps, the owner is 8,768 points behind but has 10,240 more reservoir points.

Move 6 needs reservoir weight w > 9,024 / 8,192 = 1.1016
Move 8 needs reservoir weight w < 8,768 / 10,240 = 0.8563
Therefore no fixed score + w × reservoir rule can classify both.

This is useful negative knowledge: the missing property is not simply “more stored value.” It is something about whether that value can survive, connect, and turn into score soon enough.

The sequence shape

The champion’s finishing cost after each longer owner prefix is not monotonic. It visibly contains short debt-and-repayment episodes. Most importantly, after owner move 6, champion continuation already finishes in 15—the same final cost as the complete owner game.

Matched-pair causal dissection: move 6 versus move 8

Description
Trace built material through ready reservoir to realized harvest in these two cases. Then minimally alter bridge or connecting tiles while keeping score and mass as stable as possible, and rerun takeover.
Axis
Causal identification; convertible board state.
Basis
Direct: both owner choices trail in three-step score and lead in reservoir, but their terminal ordering flips.
Rationale
This is the smallest known contrast that controls the tempting explanations. It can tell us whether topology, survival, or time-to-conversion is the missing mechanism before we invent a metric.
Downsides
Perfectly matched interventions may be impossible; conclusions must stay local to the manipulated states until a new panel confirms them.

2

Sequence labels: build, preserve, harvest

Description
Represent a short strategy as an option with a starting condition, internal policy, and stopping condition. Judge the option’s result, not whether every component move helps immediately.
Axis
Temporal unit.
Basis
Direct + external: the prefix curve contains repeated debt/repayment episodes; the options framework exists specifically for temporally extended policies.
Rationale
It explains why per-move labels call coherent play wrong and gives learning a unit that matches the observed behavior.
Downsides
We do not yet know the right episode boundaries. Choosing them prematurely can move, rather than solve, the overfitting problem.
Confidence 91%Complexity Medium
3

Minimal sufficient prefix and causal bottlenecks

Description
Find the shortest owner prefix that transfers the advantage to champion continuation, then perform splice counterfactuals only around the pivotal episodes.
Axis
Causal identification; training target.
Basis
Direct: after move 6, champion continuation already reaches the target in 15 moves. The large durable changes occur at move 2 and across moves 4–6.
Rationale
It avoids teaching from all 15 human moves when two short episodes may contain nearly all of the useful information.
Downsides
Spliced episodes can interact nonlinearly, and a single level cannot prove generality.

5

Selective deeper search on evaluator disagreement

Description
Explore a gate that invokes longer or terminal evaluation only when cheap signals materially disagree, especially at decisions with large potential target-cost impact.
Axis
Compute allocation.
Basis
Direct + external: global route expansion cost 5.14×, while value-of-computation methods formalize spending search only where it can change the decision.
Rationale
The evidence argues against deeper search everywhere but for more care at a small set of ambiguous, high-leverage states.
Downsides
The trigger needs regression-gated validation; missing an ambiguous state is more dangerous than spending extra compute unnecessarily.
Confidence 82%Complexity Medium
6

Outcome-equivalent afterstate classes

Description
Treat a candidate as successful when it reaches an afterstate with near-owner takeover cost, even if its chain differs from the owner’s.
Axis
Training target.
Basis
Direct: route-diverse search found a non-owner path with a known continuation finishing in 16, so exact imitation excludes potentially useful alternatives.
Rationale
The demonstration proves an outcome is possible; it does not prove the owner’s path is uniquely correct.
Downsides
Requires a defensible equivalence tolerance and enough exact continuation evidence; broad generation was previously expensive.
Confidence 80%Complexity Medium

Bottom line

The sharpest next investigation is move 6 versus move 8. Show exactly how each reservoir forms, survives, and converts; then change one structural feature at a time and replay. If that reveals a stable mechanism, the next abstraction should be a short build/preserve/harvest option—not another global one-move bonus.
