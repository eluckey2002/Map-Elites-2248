# Authorized conditional confirmation handoff

The owner said proceed after approving the amended budget and resumed goal.
The original frozen pipeline remains the only exploration runner. The new
`advance-resume.py` coordinator observes retained progress every 30 seconds and
waits for complete exploration plus its independent arithmetic receipt.

If exploration supplies an actual accepted selection, the coordinator commits
that literal candidate and completed exploration evidence, executes and retains
the current full regression suite, then uses `tools/new-experiment.js RESULT-0082`.
The generated template is filled and amended before confirmation; its final
freeze includes the unchanged renderer, measurement sources, selected candidate,
current suite receipt, executable closeout contract and coordinator identities.
Only then can the unchanged one-shot confirmation runner spend its reserved
17,400 games. All pairs must be persisted before the verdict.

The coordinator starts once with an exclusive, durable launch marker. Existing
candidate, protocol or confirmation outputs prevent another attempt. Unknown
checkout changes, lost execution, incomplete audits or unexpected measurement
status stop the handoff while retaining data and charges. No automatic relaunch,
seed replay, budget amendment, publication, merge or adoption occurs here.

Path B or an actual Path D stop goes directly to closeout preparation without F.
A finished confirmation still requires its report, provisional evidence record,
anchored closeout audits, live gates and actual PR review. The goal remains active
until those requirements are met. `verify-resume-closeout.js` reports the current
absence of a finished closure as pending; the negative tests reject false finishes.

Six older untracked preparation transcripts were preserved outside the checkout
with hashes and reversible locations in `preparation-archive.json`. No retained
scientific input was moved. The running raw evidence remains uncommitted until
its measurement phase has closed and its independent audit succeeds.
