# BL-0031 — Mixed-family daily puzzle (separate game idea)

## Authority

This record is intent, not evidence. Proof standing lives in the
[evidence ledger](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md); nothing here changes any record's
status or proof class.

## Desired outcome

A separate puzzle game, kept out of 2248. The kinds sampler (BL-0029) mixed the
6, 12, 24 family in with powers of two by mistake. The owner (2026-10-11) found
that mixing hard to read inside 2248, but said it "does make for a whole other
game", that "math number people would love it", and that "it has the simplicity
along with the range of complexity and easy to understand that it could be a NYT
puzzle".

The format the idea points to:

- one fixed board a day, the same for everyone, checked by hand;
- no random refills, so the whole puzzle is planning (as on the sampler's
  Dry Well board);
- the 2248 chain rule, with tiles from more than one number family (2s, 3s, and
  possibly others) on the board;
- a short result players can share;
- optionally a "new puzzle variant of the day": each day tries a different
  rule twist or level kind, so the daily puzzle doubles as a testing ground
  for ideas before they go into 2248 (the owner, 2026-10-11).

The owner's view that no game on the market combines several number families is
his own; it has not been checked.

## Acceptance criteria
