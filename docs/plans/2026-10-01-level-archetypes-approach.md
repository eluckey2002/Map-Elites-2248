# Level Archetype Collection - Plan

## Status update — 2026-10-02

The owner has revised the goal: the one strong Connections mode is enough;
there is no need to force three archetypes (PDL-026). Retain current Connection
Run and the existing Delivery references. Stop the search for a required third
family and do not require another Connections variant merely for coverage.
The original 3–5-family target is historical, not unfinished mandatory work.
Use the [workbench](../game-design/levels/archetype-workbench.md) for current
dispositions, play link, preservation and open decisions. The phases below
preserve the original approach where labeled; the current scope below controls.

Later owner feedback identifies a sustained-play material-lifecycle gap
(PDL-027): non-power-of-two results can accumulate without natural matching
refills. Exact replay confirms currently stranded residue, not inevitable
saturation. Diagnose and choose a lifecycle before continuous-mode close-out;
no gameplay fix is authorized by the report alone. Retention and removal of
the archetype quota remain unchanged.

Delivery now has two retained references, Staggered and Landing, and a
[first authoring guide](../game-design/levels/delivery-authoring-guide.md).
The owner approved capturing those lessons and returning to the other
archetypes. The owner then reported that Feeder Choice did not change play;
the current design is parked. The owner subsequently selected Turn the Board
from the existing reserves. After play, the owner found it interesting but
mixed, clarified they were not creating a setup or strategy, and agreed to
park this archetype version. Rotation remains a possible board-control
mechanic, not a retained family. PDL-026 removes the need for a replacement
concept. PDL-019, PDL-021–026 record the progression. No release, shipped-rule
change or formal operational-task acceptance follows from this scope decision.

## Objective and authority

Retain the strongest existing play experiences without a numerical archetype
quota: current Connection Run as one mode, with Delivery Staggered and Landing
retained as contrasting layouts within Delivery. Keep them playable, preserve
their rules/feedback and make their current status understandable. No additional
family or duplicate Connections representative is required.

The original request was three to five archetypes, each with a representative,
contrast and authoring guidance. PDL-026 supersedes its count and mandatory
contrast requirement for Connections. Documentation and future quality work
remain useful, but this change does not authorize new prototype development.

The owner requested conceptual design before technical experimentation, offered
to answer questions and play at checkpoints, and asked for autonomous work
between decisions. When asked whether new mechanics were allowed, the owner
answered “new mechanic.” That permits proposing mechanics; selection of the
specific concepts is the first design checkpoint.

This plan owns the archetype task. The older policy research backlog does not
direct this work. A future “continue” in this design session resumes the phase
recorded in CURRENT.md and its checkpoint, not an unrelated research question.

## Design assumptions

Preserve chain drawing, number relationships, merging, and board planning as
the recognizable core. New mechanics should change a visible choice within
that core. These are working design assumptions, open to owner correction.

Preserve strength rather than fill a count. Add another family or variation
only if a future owner-selected question warrants it. The current Connections
mode does not need another version to be worth retaining.

## Original Phase 1 — Concept selection (history)

Read the sources above and produce five concept cards, recommending three.
Each card states the proposed rule, visible decision scene, competing benefits,
relationship to the owner's baseline, persistence through refill, principal
failure risk, and a reason to reject it after play. Compare overlap explicitly.

Complete this packet without building prototypes, running seed searches,
changing the bot, or starting a technical experiment.

Checkpoint 1: the owner selects or redirects the concepts. Present a
recommendation and concrete examples. Do not ask the owner to design them.
Selection authorizes the complete prototype phase for the selected concepts.

## Original Phase 2 — Playable representatives (history)

Selection recorded 2026-10-01: the owner replied “Honestly any of them sound
fine.” Codex selected Gates, Delivery, and Feeder Choice from the recommended
set and carried the prototype phase autonomously to the first playable packet.
The [prototype README](../../prototypes/archetype-trio/README.md) records its
decision positions, verification and remaining playtest questions.

For each selected concept, construct a specific decision position before
building the surrounding level. Identify the proposed difference from the
captured build-and-harvest strategy; label the expected owner choice as a
hypothesis until observed. Establish that the competing actions have different
visible consequences and that the mechanic persists beyond the opening.

Build isolated prototypes, resolve routine rule details, verify mechanics,
and revise problems autonomously. Prepare one short play session across the
selected concepts, with an introduction and exact replay capture for each.
Use self-play and analysis to find broken or trivial positions; human play
judges whether the decision is interesting and understandable.

Checkpoint 2: the owner plays and gives a keep/revise/retire disposition.
Ask whether the choice was visible, its consequence understandable, and the
experience different from familiar play. Do not infer enjoyment from a win.

## Phase 3 — Refinement and delivery

Keep current Connection Run and the two Delivery layouts. Preserve current
play links, mechanics notes, feedback, captures and verification limits.
Delivery already has a first authoring guide. Any further refinement or
guidance should serve a named need in retained play, not the old count or a
requirement to manufacture a contrasting Connections prototype. No new work
beyond recording this scope change is selected by PDL-026.

Checkpoint 3: owner acceptance of the playable collection. This does not
silently authorize replacing shipped levels or merging to the main branch.
Handle release through the project's normal verification and delivery process.

## Autonomy between checkpoints

Proceed through all work needed to deliver the next checkpoint. Routine
implementation choices, debugging, document maintenance, verification, and
reversible adjustments within a selected concept need no additional approval.
Progress updates do not require a reply or a “continue.”

Pause only for a material change in the selected experience, a constraint that
cannot be met within the phase, or the promised human judgment. Bring the
actual problem, alternatives, and a recommendation. Finish unaffected work
while a question is pending. A selected mechanic does not need renewed
permission merely because it requires implementation work.

Do not replace a selected concept with a different mechanic without owner
input. If the concept collapses into ordinary play, explain that with a
concrete position rather than tuning indefinitely or inventing another study.

## Completion and continuity

Keep concept selection, playtest dispositions, and the next action in
CURRENT.md, linked to this plan and the concept packet. Record operational
work in the local blackboard. Proposed concepts are design material and do
not become accepted scientific claims or shipped rules by appearing here.

The concept phase is done when the owner can choose from the complete packet.
The current design selection is settled for this pass: one retained Connections
mode and the existing Delivery references, with parked ideas preserved. Do not
reopen concept selection just because fewer than three families remain.
Known native-QA limits, optional aid questions and potential future refinements
stay explicit; this is not a release or a claim that all quality work is done.
