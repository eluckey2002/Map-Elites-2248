What the evidence supports

Legal, replayable oracle witnesses on the captured corpus; broad fixed-corpus improvement by the evolved ranker; a verified bounded board archive spanning both chosen axes.

What remains open

Optimality, universal policy dominance, superiority on unseen boards, and whether every occupied cell contains a level worth shipping.

The next step is deliberate cell-filling: introduce mechanics, preserve diversity, and let human play identify what deserves to ship.
