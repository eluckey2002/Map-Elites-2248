The twist

Preview a clockwise or counterclockwise quarter-turn, then commit it for one move. Tiles settle toward the new arrow. That direction stays until you turn again.

Turns add no tiles and no points. Merges replace only the tiles they remove in the affected lanes, with values from 2 through 128.

Eight empty cells stay on the board. They are open space, not walls.

How to make a chain

Connect at least three touching tiles, including diagonals. Start with two equal numbers, then keep the same value or double it.

Tap tiles or drag a path. The last tile becomes the sum, then gravity acts. Preview before committing. Undo turns and merges freely.
