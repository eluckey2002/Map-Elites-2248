    Make room

    A merge that makes a tile of 2,048 or more earns one removal (bank up to 3). Removing a tile costs a move.

    How it works

    Each puzzle has three connections, one at a time. Start and end a chain on ① and ②, and make the exact sum.

    The chain's last tile keeps the sum. In these puzzles the tile you make is part of what comes next, and its marker moves down with it if it falls.

    Chains start with two equal tiles, then go equal or double, at least three tiles, diagonals count. New tiles are 2 to 128. Start over gives the same board and the same new tiles.
