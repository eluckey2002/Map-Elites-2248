Make the order from one ingredient.

An order like 512 = 4 × 128 is filled by one chain of exactly four 128s. A chain that mixes values, or uses too many, doesn't count. Ringed tiles are the ingredient.

There is no score. Fill every order before the moves run out.

How to make a chain

Click touching tiles, diagonals included: start with two equal tiles, then equal or double. Use at least three. The last tile you click keeps the sum. Preview, clear and undo are free; retries use the same board and refills.
