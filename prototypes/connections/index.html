WHAT COUNTS

Two ends. One chain.

A & B travel with their tiles

Start at A1 and finish at A2, or reverse them. For B, start at B1 (16) and finish at B2 (64).

Unfinished marked tiles can only be used as the two ends of their own connection. Clear beneath them to move them with gravity.

C stays on the board

Start at C1 and finish at C2, or reverse them. The dashed positions stay fixed; the numbers inside can change.

Same chaining rules

Connect at least three touching tiles, including diagonals. The first two must match; each following tile must be equal or double.

The last tile becomes the sum. Ordinary chains can prepare the board. A completed goal stays checked off.

Tap or drag. Preview and undo freely. Score is not the objective.
