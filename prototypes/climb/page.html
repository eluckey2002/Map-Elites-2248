Rules
  • Tap touching tiles to make a chain. Start with two the same, then each next tile is the same or double.
  • Tap the last tile again, or Merge, to merge. Tap the tile before the last to step back.
  • The merged tile is the exact sum. Bonus: ×1.5 at 3 tiles, ×2 at 5, ×3 at 7, ×5 at 9 or more.
  • New tiles fall from the values shown in the strip. When you climb, the smallest value stops falling and a bigger one starts.
  • Tiles smaller than every falling value are outlined. If one can no longer merge, it gets a dashed outline and "!". One move later, if it still can't merge, it turns to stone.
  • Stone can't be chained and holds up the tiles above it.
  • Board 5 wide, 7 tall. Shortest chain 2. Undo goes back one move. The game ends when no chain is left.
