## Question

On small generated 2248 puzzles where reachability can be enumerated exactly,
does `budget tightness × chain-length dependence` occupy four mechanically
distinct regions—relaxed/short, relaxed/long, tight/short, and tight/long—with
replayable examples from varied starting boards?

## Why this is being asked

`RESULT-0017` established diversity in two **policy-behavior** descriptors. It
did not describe puzzle instances. The current goal asks whether two
solver-independent reachability measurements can organize generated puzzles
without being renamed difficulty, fun, policy quality, or fitness.

The definitions are frozen:

- **Budget tightness:** exact minimum moves required to reach the score target,
  divided by the allowed two moves.
- **Chain-length dependence:** the smallest maximum-chain-length cap under
  which the target remains exactly reachable within the move budget.

The second definition is a counterfactual over all legal play, not a statistic
from one solver trace.

## Shape of the run

One deterministic exact generated-corpus run. It is not policy evaluation,
MAP-Elites policy search, a human study, a shipped-level study, or a claim
about naturally occurring region frequency.

Every subject is a blocker-free 3x3 board generated by the real seeded initial
deal, with `minChain: 3`, `tileScale: 1`, and two allowed moves. The exact
position-aware action enumerator and frozen spawn stream are the same seams
used by `solver/exact-score.js`. Search retains the best reachable cumulative
score for every move count and every chain-length cap.

## Frozen region construction

Each region owns a disjoint seed block. Seeds are examined in ascending order
and the first four exact qualifying puzzles are retained. The target is
constructed by a rule frozen before the board is seen:

- `relaxed-short`: the best one-move score under chain cap 3.
- `relaxed-long`: one point above the best one-move score under cap 4, only
  when an unrestricted one-move action clears that threshold.
- `tight-short`: one point above the best one-move cap-3 score, only when the
  two-move cap-3 envelope clears that threshold.
- `tight-long`: one point above the larger of the unrestricted one-move score
  and the two-move cap-4 score, only when the unrestricted two-move envelope
  clears that threshold.

These rules make the intended counterfactual visible without selecting a
single solver policy. They do not estimate how common any region is.

## Checks, classified before outcomes are assigned

### C1 — exact-repeat negative control (PASS / FAIL)

The qualification fixture must reproduce the same envelope, descriptors, and
witnesses under the same level and seed. Any nondeterminism is `FAIL` and stops
the corpus.

### C2 — planted bad-descriptor and missing-witness controls (PASS / FAIL)

The real instance verifier must accept a recomputed qualification instance,
reject that same instance after its descriptor is changed, and reject it after
its minimum-cap witness is removed. If either bad twin passes, C2 is `FAIL` and
the corpus does not run.

### C3 — registration, identity, and source closure (PASS / FAIL)

The runner must refuse unregistered execution. A reportable artifact must bind
to the committed RESULT-0029 protocol, hash to its body independently of the
registration stamp, and record every behavior-carrying source frozen above.
Any mismatch is `FAIL`.

### C4 — bounded-search standing remains honest (PASS / FAIL)

The exact search has a 250,000 expanded-node cap per starting board. A cap hit
must be recorded as `UNKNOWN` and cannot become unreachable, non-qualifying
exact evidence, or a selected puzzle. The permanent cap fixture must enforce
that distinction.

### P1 — four-region structural separation

- `SUPPORTED` — all four regions retain at least four exact instances, at
  least 12 distinct starting-board identities appear across the 16 selected
  instances, and every selected instance has strict counterfactual bounds
  below its target for both fewer moves and the next-shorter chain cap.
- `INCONCLUSIVE` — any condition above is unmet, including search exhaustion,
  insufficient qualifying seeds, or board-identity collapse.

No alternate seeds, relaxed threshold, or post-run region definition is
permitted.

### P2 — puzzle-instance identity and paired evidence (PASS / FAIL)

Every selected row must record one identity binding its rules, seed, initial
board, frozen spawn stream, target, and move budget. Descriptor values,
counterfactual maxima, and both witnesses must be evaluated on that same
identity. Cross-seed or cross-target comparisons cannot stand in for a pair.

### P3 — witness replay and full recomputation (PASS / FAIL)

For every selected puzzle, the minimum-moves witness and minimum-cap witness
must replay through the frozen real transition seam to the recorded target.
The verifier must then regenerate screening, selection, descriptor values,
representatives, and disposition from the frozen seed blocks and agree byte
for byte on the canonical evidence body.

### P4 — scoped descriptor disposition and inspectable extremes

- `SUPPORTED` when P1 is supported and P2–P3 pass: promote both descriptors
  only for exact micro-puzzle instance maps in the measured scope.
- `INCONCLUSIVE` otherwise: reject both descriptors for that scoped use until
  a new registered design repairs the observed failure.

The report must render the initial board, target, descriptor coordinates,
strict counterfactuals, and replayable witness chains for one representative
from every occupied region. It must explicitly preserve these exclusions:
human difficulty, fun, player preference, solver fitness, policy-behavior
axes, shipped-level changes, game-rule changes, and descriptor-catalogue
expansion.

## Budget and stopping rules

1. Commit this protocol, runner, verifier, exact instrument, seed registry,
   and permanent tests before opening any reportable seed.
2. Run focused controls and the repository experiment gate; stop on a new
   failure.
3. Run the corpus exactly once. The runner refuses overwrite.
4. Run the verifier exactly once against that artifact; deterministic
   recomputation is verification, not a second sample.
5. Preserve every opened seed and every `UNKNOWN` standing in the artifact.
6. Do not extend a seed block, rerun with different caps, or tune construction
   after observing occupancy.

Hard limits: 192 screened boards, 16 selected instances, two moves per puzzle,
250,000 expanded nodes per board. No external calls or human rating.

## Instrument bound

Load-bearing evidence is exact reachability within the frozen 3x3/two-move
scope, strict counterfactual maxima, puzzle identities, and witness replay.
Runtime and search-node counts are diagnostics. A node-cap hit is `UNKNOWN`,
not evidence against reachability or against either descriptor.

## Adoption is a separate decision

A supported result authorizes only a source-pinned decision to use these axes
for exact micro-puzzle instance maps. It does not replace the existing policy
behavior axes, choose a solver objective, rank shipped levels, ship a level,
or claim difficulty or fun. Applying the axes to larger boards requires a new
goal that preserves bounded or heuristic standing honestly.
